Privacy Policy – Parkeringsassistent P40

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Independent app: Parkeringsassistent P40 is an independent utility and is not developed, approved, supported or operated by SmartPark, Trondheim Parking, Trondheim Municipality or the parking operator.
Privacy is built into the design: The app does not use advertising or cross-app/site tracking. The SmartPark session is stored in Apple Keychain, optional vehicle photos remain local, and the app does not operate its own server database of users' P40 account data. SmartPark tokens, phone numbers and SMS codes are not sent to Apple Watch. The app and its extensions also include Apple's privacy manifests to declare required system-API use.

1. About the app

Parkeringsassistent P40 is a utility for people who already have access to the relevant P40 parking scheme. It helps the user access their existing parking account, select a vehicle, display vehicle information and view relevant parking status.

2. Personal data and other information processed

The app may process the following information in order to provide its functionality:

  • Phone number – used for authentication with the underlying parking service.
  • User or account identification – used to identify and authenticate the user's existing parking account.
  • Vehicle registration number – used to identify the vehicle a parking permit applies to and may be used to retrieve technical vehicle information.
  • Vehicle information – make, model and colour may be retrieved from the Norwegian Public Roads Administration technical vehicle API through the developer's Cloudflare Worker. The registration number is sent by the app in the JSON body of an HTTPS POST request, not in the Worker URL. The Norwegian Public Roads Administration API key is not embedded in the app.
  • Optional vehicle photo – a photo selected by the user for easier visual identification. The photo is processed locally on the user's device.
  • Precise location (optional) – is processed locally before a manual booking through I'M AT WORK and before booking started from the CarPlay widget. If the phone is safely more than 1 km from the fixed zone around Sluppenvegen 17A, or the distance cannot be verified, the user must explicitly choose Book P40 before anything is sent to SmartPark. Manual booking does not arm the two-hour safety follow-up. Location is also used after a scheduled automatic booking for arrival and safety checks. Optional P40 Arrival Start booking is triggered by the user's Apple Shortcuts automation and does not require an additional in-app GPS check before booking. GPS coordinates are not sent to the parking service, the developer or other third-party services and are not stored as parking history.

3. Purpose of processing

The information is used only to provide app functionality, including to:

  • authenticate the user with the parking service
  • identify the user's existing parking account
  • select or register the correct vehicle
  • retrieve and display technical vehicle information
  • display an optional vehicle photo selected by the user
  • check the relevant parking permit
  • display parking status and remaining time
  • locally verify whether a manual or CarPlay-originated booking may be sent when the iPhone is outside the fixed 1 km zone, and support post-booking scheduled-arrival/safety checks
  • display relevant information through Live Activities, the Lock Screen, Dynamic Island and a compatible paired Apple Watch when supported by the operating system

The information is not used for advertising, marketing or cross-app or cross-site tracking.

Controller and legal bases

For processing where Parkeringsassistent P40 determines the purpose and means, Roar Skjønberg is the data controller. Contact details are provided at the end of this page.

  • Core functions requested by the user, including local storage of the selected vehicle and processing necessary to check or request P40 parking, are processed to the extent necessary to provide the service requested by the user under Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Optional location-based automation is based on consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Consent is expressed by actively enabling the feature and granting the required iOS location permission. It may be withdrawn by disabling the automation or revoking location access.
  • Local technical diagnostics and security data are processed for P40's legitimate interest in providing a secure, stable and troubleshootable app under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Processing is limited and mainly local and excludes the SmartPark token, phone number and SMS code from the diagnostic event timeline.
  • Optional vehicle photos and voluntary diagnostic sharing occur only after an active user choice. Diagnostic sharing with the developer is based on consent and never happens automatically.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that took place before withdrawal. Some optional features may then be unavailable, such as the two-hour safety follow-up with a release option without the required location access.

SmartPark/Giant Leap, the parking operator, Cloudflare and the Norwegian Public Roads Administration may have their own roles and purposes for information they receive through their services. Their processing is also governed by their own terms and privacy notices.

Parkeringsassistent P40 does not make solely automated individual decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significantly affect the user within the meaning of Article 22 GDPR. The built-in two-hour feature is user-enabled technical automation and is fail-safe: parking is kept whenever the safety requirements cannot be verified.

4. What is stored locally?

Parkeringsassistent P40 does not maintain its own server database of users' personal data.

The following information may be stored locally on the user's iPhone:

  • Authentication token – stored securely in Apple Keychain so the user can remain signed in.
  • Registration numbers – vehicles added or selected by the user may be stored locally.
  • Vehicle information – make, model and colour may be cached locally to reduce unnecessary lookups.
  • Optional vehicle photo – a compressed copy is stored locally and associated with the registration number. It is not sent to the developer, SmartPark, Cloudflare, the Norwegian Public Roads Administration or other third-party services.
  • Technical session information – limited information may be stored temporarily to handle a pending or uncertain parking request.
  • Local automation state – the registration number selected as the dedicated Automatic P40 vehicle, the time a new P40 booking was actually created, the vehicle registration number it applies to, the technical SmartPark identifier used to bind any later user-confirmed stop to that same parking session, the time the 1 km arrival monitor was confirmed armed, any time monitoring continuity was lost, any observed region-entry signal and GPS-verified arrival time, and the time and limited result status of the latest safety check may be stored locally. These are identifiers, status and event timestamps, not GPS coordinates. The state is retained only while relevant to the current automation flow and expires no later than 24 hours after booking. The GPS coordinate itself is not stored as parking history.
  • Built-in two-hour safety check – after a confirmed time-based pre-booking, P40 may register a local background task that can run no earlier than two hours after booking. The task uses the local automation state already stored by the app and does not introduce any new sharing of location or parking data. iOS decides when the background task actually runs. If execution is delayed, P40 may show a local notification with relevant timing information. The notification is created on the device and introduces no new data transfer.
  • Local diagnostics – timestamps and results for status checks, successful SmartPark calls, background checks, session confirmations, Apple Watch/WatchConnectivity status, status retry, Health Check and technical errors. To measure session lifetime, the app may also store locally the time a sign-in session was created or first observed, the last authenticated verification, last keep-alive and the number of successful session confirmations, including confirmations at startup and when the app enters the foreground, plus confirmed session end time/reason. The token, phone number and SMS code are not stored in this session measurement. The app may also keep a limited timeline of up to 30 diagnostic events for up to 7 days. These data are used for troubleshooting and stored locally.
  • Language preference and optional translated UI cache – the selected app language is stored locally. On supported devices, additional translated UI text may also be cached locally.

Vehicle photos are selected using Apple's system photo picker, and the app only receives access to the photo selected by the user.

The phone number is used during authentication with the parking service and is not intended to be stored permanently by Parkeringsassistent P40.

Parking status and validity are retrieved from the underlying parking service. The app does not create its own parking-history database. The limited diagnostic event timeline is a troubleshooting aid and is not intended to be a parking history.

Diagnostic data are not sent automatically to the developer. The user may choose “Copy diagnostics” or the built-in “Send diagnostics to developer” flow. Before diagnostics are copied, used by the on-device AI feature or shared with the developer, known vehicle registration numbers are masked. The SmartPark token, phone number, SMS code and other sign-in credentials are excluded.

Apple Watch companion app: If the user installs Parkeringsassistent P40 on a paired Apple Watch, the iPhone may send required status information through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. This may include P40 state, registration number, vehicle description, expiry time, last verification time and the selected app language. If the user has enabled the option to hide the registration number on Lock Screen and status surfaces, the masked display value is sent to Apple Watch. When an additional translated language is used, the local UI translation cache may also be synchronized to the Watch.

SmartPark token, phone number, SMS code and other sign-in credentials are not sent to Apple Watch. SmartPark authentication and parking logic remain on the iPhone.

CarPlay/StandBy widget: The widget reads local P40 status from the app's App Group, including parking state, vehicle description, expiry and latest verified time. Depending on the user's privacy setting, the registration number is shown or replaced by a masked display value. The widget does not receive the SmartPark token, SMS code, phone number or GPS coordinates and does not make its own SmartPark or vehicle-lookup network requests. Book and Release open the main app, which performs fresh verification before any SmartPark write. An exact audio/connection-name match is not a security requirement.

AI-assisted diagnostics explanation

On supported devices, Parkeringsassistent P40 may use Apple Foundation Models – SystemLanguageModel.default (on-device) to explain technical diagnostics in more understandable language. The AI feature is optional and is started by the user from … → Diagnostics. The exact system-model version is selected by iOS, so the app does not claim a more specific model identity.

Before diagnostics are used by the AI feature, the app filters the text locally. Known vehicle registration numbers are masked, and the SmartPark token, phone number, SMS code and other sign-in credentials are not included. The explanation is generated locally on the iPhone when Apple's on-device model is available.

The result is clearly labeled AI-generated explanation, and the app identifies the model/API used as Apple Foundation Models – SystemLanguageModel.default (on-device). The app may also use the privacy-filtered Health Check summary and local event timeline when the user chooses “What happened? Explain with AI”. The recommendation about whether diagnostics should be shared is separate, deterministic P40 logic and is not generated by AI. AI can be wrong and the result is not confirmation that parking is valid. If status is uncertain, verify it directly in SmartPark.

Diagnostics are never sent automatically to the developer. If P40's deterministic recommendation indicates that sharing may be useful, the user can first review the exact privacy-filtered text and then explicitly choose to send it to the developer.

5. Language and local translation

Norwegian and English are built-in, reviewed app languages. New users start directly in Norwegian or English when that is the iPhone language. For another iPhone language, the app starts in English. If both Apple Foundation Models and Apple Translation report support for the phone language on that device, the user is offered that language. The interface translation itself uses Apple Translation – on-device translation model. Translated UI text may be cached locally on the device.

If a required translation language is not ready, iOS may ask the user to download the relevant language model. Parkeringsassistent P40 does not send these interface strings to the developer's own translation server. English is used as the fallback when a translated string is unavailable.

Privacy, terms and support are maintained only in Norwegian and English. Additional app languages open the English versions of these pages.

6. Third-party services

Parkeringsassistent P40 communicates directly with the underlying parking service (SmartPark/Giant Leap) to perform functions requested by the user.

Depending on the function, this may include the phone number and verification information used during sign-in, the authentication token, vehicle registration number, parking start date, active parking/permit information and technical permit identifiers. When the user stops an active P40 parking session, the relevant permit identifier is sent together with a stop command. GPS location is used only locally on the iPhone by the optional automation feature and is not sent to SmartPark/Giant Leap as part of arrival or stop verification.

This information is used only for app functionality such as authentication and checking, creating or stopping parking sessions. As part of the technical communication, the app may also send limited client metadata such as device type, operating-system/client version and language/locale. Information sent to the parking service may be processed and stored by the service provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

For vehicle lookups, the registration number is sent through a Cloudflare Worker as a technical intermediary to the Norwegian Public Roads Administration API. The app uses the response to display make, model and colour.

This API provides technical vehicle information, not owner information. Parkeringsassistent P40 does not request names, addresses or other owner details from this API.

Persistent Workers Logs are disabled for the developer's Cloudflare Worker. Cloudflare and the Norwegian Public Roads Administration may still process technical information required to deliver and secure their services under their own terms and privacy policies.

Cloudflare states that its services may involve processing outside the EEA. Where a transfer requires additional safeguards, Cloudflare's current Data Processing Addendum describes the use of mechanisms including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and other applicable transfer safeguards.

7. Data the app does not collect for its own purposes

  • contacts
  • photos or videos sent to the developer's servers or third-party services; an optional vehicle photo is stored locally only
  • audio recordings
  • health or fitness data
  • payment-card or banking information
  • advertising identifiers
  • data for targeted advertising
  • vehicle-owner information

Precise location may be processed locally on the iPhone both for optional P40 automation and as a one-shot check before a manual booking through “I'M AT WORK”. If the phone is safely outside the 1 km zone, or location cannot be verified, the user must explicitly choose Book P40 before anything is sent to SmartPark. Manual booking does not arm the two-hour safety follow-up. Location is not transmitted to the developer, SmartPark or other third-party services by this feature and is not used for tracking, profiling or advertising.

The app contains no advertising and does not use personal data for tracking.

8. Security

Information that must be stored locally is handled using available iOS security mechanisms. Protected authentication information is stored in Apple Keychain. Communication with external services uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

The API key used for vehicle lookups is not stored in the app on the user's device. Vehicle requests are routed through an intermediary so the API key is not distributed with the app.

9. User responsibility

Parkeringsassistent P40 is a utility. The user remains responsible for checking that the correct vehicle, parking area and any required permit are actually registered.

Information shown in the app is not a guarantee that parking is valid if the underlying parking service has not confirmed the registration.

10. Rights, access and deletion

Where the conditions for the relevant right are met, the user may have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Consent may be withdrawn at any time.

Much of the information processed by P40 exists only locally on the user's device and is therefore not available to the developer. Signing out removes the SmartPark session from Apple Keychain. Vehicles and vehicle photos can be deleted in the app. Uninstalling the app removes data stored in the app's local container.

Local automation state expires no later than 24 hours after the relevant booking. The local diagnostic event timeline contains at most 30 events and is retained for up to 7 days. Session-lifetime and preference data remain local until replaced by newer data, deleted through a relevant app function, or removed with the app's local data.

If the user has voluntarily sent privacy-filtered diagnostics to the developer, the user may contact the developer to request access, correction or deletion of material the developer has actually received and still retains.

Information already processed by SmartPark, the parking operator, Cloudflare, the Norwegian Public Roads Administration or other third parties must also be handled through the relevant service's privacy procedures.

Users have the right to lodge a complaint about personal-data processing with the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet.

11. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated if app functionality or the processing of personal data changes. If the app later processes new categories of personal data, this policy and relevant App Store information will be updated.
